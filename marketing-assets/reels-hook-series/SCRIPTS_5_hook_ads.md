# Zoree TMS — 5 hook-ad reel scripts

Direct-to-camera format, under 45 seconds each. Spoken register — short sentences,
contractions on. On-screen captions are separate from speech and carry the hook on mute.
Every claim below comes from your own deck, demo videos, or launch film — nothing invented.

## Script 1 — "The cheapest carrier found itself"

**Hook (0:00–0:03)**
> What if every load found its cheapest carrier — by itself?

**Stakes (0:03–0:08)**
> Most shippers rate-shop maybe one load in ten. The rest ship on whoever's familiar. That's money out the door on every lane.

**Proof (0:08–0:14)**
> When Gorilla Glue put this in, nearly all their prepaid freight was on a single carrier. No comparison. No visibility.

**Mechanism (0:14–0:32)**
> Here's how it works in Zoree. Pick an order. Hit Plan. It shops every contracted carrier — TL, LTL, partial — live rates side by side. Same-lane orders get combined into one truck automatically, and it shows you the saving.

**Payoff (0:32–0:40)**
> Every load, cheapest compliant carrier, with the comparison recorded. Not once a week. Every time.

**CTA (0:40–0:45)**
> Watch the full demo — link's in my profile. zoree.io.

**Captions:** 1. "Cheapest carrier. Automatically." · 2. "9 of 10 loads never get shopped" · 3. "Every carrier. Side by side." · 4. "Two orders → one truck" · 5. "zoree.io/zoree-demo"

**Shot list:** talking head for hook/stakes → screen recording of Plan + rate results → consolidation popup → back to camera for payoff/CTA.

**Cover frame:** freeze on the rate-comparison screen with the hook text overlaid.
