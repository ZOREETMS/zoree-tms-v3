You place one online order. Six different systems touch it before it reaches your door.

Imagine ordering a pair of shoes online.

• The Order Management System (OMS) decides where the order should be fulfilled.
• The ERP — SAP, Oracle, Odoo — records the sale and its financial impact.
• The Warehouse Management System (WMS) guides picking, packing, and dispatch.
• The Transportation Management System (TMS) selects the carrier and manages the shipment.
• Planning tools check inventory and calculate replenishment.
• Procurement platforms manage suppliers and purchasing.

Here's the catch: the TMS is where the physical world meets all of them. It's the moment your data becomes a truck on a road.

That's why we built Zoree TMS as the execution layer that keeps the whole chain in sync:

→ Connects to your ERP (Oracle Fusion via OIC) so orders flow in without re-keying
→ Shops rates across carriers automatically (SMC3, PC*MILER) and picks least-cost
→ Gives carriers their own portal to accept tenders and bid on lanes
→ Audits every freight invoice against the agreed rate — automatically
→ Feeds live dashboards, so planning sees reality, not last week's spreadsheet
→ ZoreeAI lets your team ask questions and take action in plain English

A connected supply chain isn't one giant system. It's specialized systems working as one — and the TMS is the one that makes it physical.
