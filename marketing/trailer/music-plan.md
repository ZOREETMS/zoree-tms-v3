# Music Plan — Zoree Launch Trailer

## Direction
Modern cinematic-corporate hybrid: atmospheric low strings and synth pads that build into a confident mid-tempo pulse. Zimmer-scale *atmosphere* (space, low-end warmth, patient builds) without drama, braams, or epic-trailer clichés. Inspirational, forward-moving, restrained.

Reference feel (do not copy): Apple keynote films, AWS re:Invent openers, Salesforce brand films.

## Structure mapped to the edit (85s)
| Section | Time | Music |
|---|---|---|
| Atmosphere | 0:00–0:12 | Warm sub pad + sparse piano note, distant pulse enters at 0:08 |
| Tension | 0:12–0:20 | Low string ostinato, filtered percussion tightens |
| The breath | 0:20–0:22 | Near-silence — everything drops |
| Reveal | 0:22–0:26 | Single clean downbeat on logo, pad blooms |
| Momentum | 0:26–0:56 | Mid-tempo pulse (~100 BPM), warm synth arps, muted rhythmic strings; small lifts on PLAN and EXECUTE beats |
| Open sky | 0:56–1:06 | Chords widen for TRACK section — more air, less percussion |
| Peak | 1:06–1:18 | Fullest arrangement under AI section — still restrained, no choir |
| Resolve | 1:18–1:25 | Strip to sustained warm chord + piano, single final note under logo |

2. **Musicbed** — filters: Cinematic > Minimal, mood: Determined/Hopeful. Higher-end, best for brand films.

4. **PremiumBeat** — "corporate cinematic minimal."

Search keywords that find this sound: *minimal cinematic pulse, warm analog pads, corporate documentary, hopeful build, restrained percussion, technology brand film.*

## Rules
- No royalty-free "epic trailer" tracks (braams, hits, choirs).
- No EDM drops, no ukulele-corporate, no whistling.
- Track must have a natural low point at ~25% (for the logo breath) or be editable to create one.
- License: web + broadcast + paid social, perpetual.
