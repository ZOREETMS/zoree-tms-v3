# Editing Plan — Zoree Launch Trailer

Master: 4K 16:9, 23.976fps, 85s. Deliverables: 4K master, 1080p web, 1:1 and 9:16 social cutdowns (later).

## Editing grammar (Apple-commercial rules)
1. **Nothing is static.** Every UI clip gets a move: 3–5% slow push, lateral crop pan, or animated crop zoom to the element being narrated.
2. **Cut on motion or on beat.** B-roll cuts land on music downbeats; UI cuts land on interaction moments (click, success state).
3. **UI in device frames** for establishing shots (subtle 3D-tilted laptop/browser frame, soft shadow, brand-navy backdrop); **full-bleed crops** for detail moments.
4. **Highlighting:** animated rounded-rect spotlight (dim surroundings 30%, 200ms ease) on the narrated element — best rate row, tender card, AI response. Never arrows, never cursors circling.
5. **Transitions:** hard cuts 90% of the time. Two signature transitions only: light-flare wipe (B-roll → UI) at 0:26, and a speed-ramp whip on the truck at 0:53. No dissolves between UI screens.
6. **Text:** single word, brand typeface, lower-third-free. Fade-in 200ms, hold ≤2s: PLAN · EXECUTE · TRACK · OPTIMIZE · AI ASSISTANT. Kinetic tracking-in on letters, subtle.
7. **Speed ramps:** demo UI captured at 1× is retimed 130–200% during non-critical motion; success moments (tender sent, AI confirmation) play at 100% and hold 12 extra frames.

## Sound design
- Narration leads; music ducked −6dB under VO.
- UI foley: soft clicks, single confirm chime on U4 (subtle, not app-store-y).
- B-roll ambience: distant port horns, highway wash, warehouse room tone — low, felt not heard.
- 1.5s of near-silence before logo drop at 0:22 (the "Apple breath").

## Color
- B-roll: warm highlights (+8 gold), lifted navy shadows, gentle halation.
- UI: untouched color accuracy (product truth), +3% contrast only.
- Consistent navy backdrop (#0B1220 region of brand palette) behind framed UI.

## Quality gate (before lock)
- No blank frames, no cursor wander, no readable fake text in AI shots.
- Reject any AI clip: bad wheels/hands/faces/text/logos/warped containers/crane physics.
- Legibility check at 1080p mobile: every highlighted UI element readable.
- Trailer must END on CTA to zoree.io demo — the existing demo stays the deep-dive.
