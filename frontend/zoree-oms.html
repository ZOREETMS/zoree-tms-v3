📦 TMS Plan — Tender Accepted
🔍Availability CheckStage 2 → 3
Verifying on-hand inventory against ordered quantities. Confirmed stock will be reserved for this order.
📤Release to TMSStage 3 → 4
TMS / OTM Planning: Shipment request sent as Unplanned. OTM rates carriers, selects optimal option by cost and SLA, and creates a Planned Shipment with carrier assignment, pickup/delivery dates, and freight rate.
📋Confirm Shipment DetailsStage 5 → 6
TMS has assigned a carrier and planned this shipment. Enter BOL & PRO numbers below to generate shipping docs and release to warehouse for picking.
🔎Pick OrderStage 6 → 7
Stage 7 — Picking: Picker retrieves items from bin locations per pick list. Confirm each line when picked.
📦Pack OrderStage 7 → 8
Stage 8 — Packing: Items packed into cartons. Enter carton count, final weight, and generate shipping labels.
🚪Dock AssignmentStage 8 → 9
Stage 9 — Staged: Assign packed pallet(s) to a dock door. Carrier will be spotted at the assigned dock for loading.
🚛Load & ShipStage 9 → 10
Stage 10 — Shipped: Cartons loaded and scanned to trailer. BOL signed by driver. TMS updated to In Transit. Inventory deducted.
✅Proof of DeliveryStage 10 → 11
Stage 11 — Delivered / POD: Capture proof of delivery. Triggers EDI 210 freight invoice generation.
